Small reminders to keep what matters close

Smrti (स्मृति) means memory. It sends you quotes, motivation and dialogues with your own pictures, right on your lock screen.

Download for Android
Android 9 or newer · free

What it does

🔔

Pops up like a message

A banner over your home screen and other apps, and a card on the lock screen. Tap it to read the quote inside the app.

⏱️

Your rhythm

Every minute, 10 minutes, hourly, every 5 hours or anything from 1 minute to 7 days. Quiet hours included.

🖼️

Your pictures

Add photos or whole phone albums. Show them at random or one after another in series.

✍️

Your words

Add your own quotes with a live preview and emoji picker, or import a JSON file of quotes.

🌑

True black

Built for OLED screens, with a floating bottom bar and swipe between tabs.

🔒

Private

Everything stays on your phone. No account, no ads, no tracking.

Install

  1. Download the APK with the button above, on your phone.
  2. Open it. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser or file manager.
  3. Allow notifications when Smrti asks.
  4. For exact short intervals, open Settings → Precise timing in the app and allow it. Set battery usage to Unrestricted for the best timing.
